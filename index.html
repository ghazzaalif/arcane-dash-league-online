Rules

How to play

Draft four runners for each team. The draft happens in two open waves using a snake order.

Play four races. Before each race, every team chooses one runner that has not raced yet.

On a turn, roll and move. Every runner has a unique power that changes movement, reactions, or hazards.

The Calm Course has no special spaces. The Chaos Circuit contains boosts, setbacks, and trip spaces.

The first two runners to cross space 30 score points. Later races are worth more. Highest total after four races wins.
