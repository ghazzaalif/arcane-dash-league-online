Arcane Dash League
Original characters, maps, code and synthesized sound.

How to play

Draft

Every team drafts four runners in two waves. The pick order snakes: it reverses after each pass, and wave two starts with the next team.

Race

Before each of the four races, every team sends one runner that hasn't raced yet. On your turn, roll and move. Each runner's power bends the rules in its own way.

Tracks

Races 1 and 3 are calm tracks with no special spaces. Races 2 and 4 are chaos tracks with boost, setback and trip spaces.

Scoring

The first two runners to reach the finish (space 30) score. Points grow each race:

The runners
